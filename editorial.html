Editorial Standards

Our commitment to honest, accurate and responsible journalism.

📅 Last updated: June 2026

Our Editorial Philosophy

At Naija Insights, journalism is a public service. We believe Nigerians deserve access to credible, well-researched and balanced news that helps them make informed decisions about their lives, communities and country.

Founded in 2025 by Adigun Simon, Naija Insights was built on the principle that good journalism must be truthful, fair, independent and accountable. These are not just aspirations — they are the standards we hold ourselves to every day.

Our Core Principles

✅ Accuracy

We verify all facts before publication. We do not publish unverified claims, rumours or speculation as fact.

⚖️ Fairness

We give all sides of a story the opportunity to be heard. We do not favour any political party, religion or ethnic group.

🔍 Independence

Our editorial decisions are made independently. Advertisers, sponsors and external parties have no influence over our content.

📢 Accountability

We correct our mistakes promptly and transparently. When we get something wrong, we say so clearly and correct the record.

🤝 Humanity

We treat all people with dignity. We are sensitive to the impact our reporting can have on individuals and communities.

⚡ Timeliness

We deliver news fast but never at the expense of accuracy. Speed without truth is not journalism.

Verification Standards

Before any article is published on Naija Insights, our editorial team follows these verification steps:

  • Every factual claim is verified against at least one credible source
  • Breaking news is clearly labelled and updated as more information becomes available
  • We do not publish anonymous allegations without substantial corroborating evidence
  • All quotes are attributed to named, identifiable sources wherever possible
  • Images and videos are verified before publication to prevent misinformation

Corrections Policy

We take accuracy seriously. If an error is discovered in a published article, we will:

  • Correct the article as quickly as possible
  • Add a clear correction notice at the top or bottom of the article explaining what was changed and why
  • Never silently delete or alter articles without acknowledgement
  • Contact the person or organisation affected by the error where appropriate

To report an error, please contact us via WhatsApp at +234 704 580 3223.

Conflicts of Interest

Naija Insights is committed to editorial independence. Our journalists and contributors must:

  • Disclose any personal or financial interest in a story they are covering
  • Not accept gifts, payments or favours from sources or subjects of coverage
  • Not use their position at Naija Insights to advance personal or commercial interests
  • Avoid covering stories where they have a direct personal relationship with the subject

Sponsored Content & Advertising

Naija Insights clearly distinguishes between editorial content and paid or sponsored content. Any sponsored article, advertorial or paid partnership will be clearly labelled as such.

Advertisers do not have any influence over our editorial content, news coverage or article selection. Our coverage of any company or organisation is never influenced by whether they advertise with us.

Sensitive Topics

When covering sensitive topics — including conflict, crime, health crises, religious matters or ethnic issues — we apply the following guidelines:

  • We avoid language that inflames tensions or promotes division
  • We are careful not to identify victims of crimes or disasters without their consent or without public interest justification
  • We report on security matters responsibly, avoiding content that could endanger lives
  • We treat mental health stories with sensitivity and in accordance with responsible reporting guidelines

User Generated Content & Comments

Naija Insights welcomes reader comments and engagement. We moderate comments to ensure they meet our community standards. Comments that contain hate speech, misinformation, threats or personal attacks will be removed.

We are not responsible for the opinions expressed in reader comments, but we take our role as a platform seriously and will act when content is harmful.
