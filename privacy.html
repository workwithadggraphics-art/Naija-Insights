Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

📅 Last updated: June 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Naija Insights. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data with transparency and care. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you visit our website at naijainsights.vercel.app.

By using our website, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Email address — when you subscribe to our newsletter or Daily Briefing
  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent on site, articles read and view counts
  • Comments — name and comment text when you post a comment on an article
  • Device information — browser type, operating system and screen size for optimising your experience

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as financial information, government ID numbers or location data.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send you our newsletter and breaking news alerts (if you subscribed)
  • Improve our website content and user experience
  • Analyse which articles and categories are most popular
  • Display your comments on articles
  • Respond to enquiries sent via WhatsApp

Newsletter Subscription

When you subscribe to our Daily Briefing newsletter, your email address is stored securely with Mailchimp, our email service provider. You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email we send you.

We will never sell, rent or share your email address with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience. Cookies we use include:

  • Essential cookies — required for the website to function properly
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how visitors interact with our site
  • Preference cookies — remember which articles you have already viewed

You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Disabling cookies may affect some features of the website.

Third Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate our website:

  • Firebase (Google) — our database and authentication provider
  • Mailchimp — our newsletter and email marketing platform
  • Vercel — our website hosting provider
  • ImgBB / Cloudinary — image hosting for article cover photos

Each of these providers has their own privacy policies governing how they handle data.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time
  • Request removal of your comments

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us via WhatsApp.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
